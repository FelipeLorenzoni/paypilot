# Product Overview

PayPilot is a personal finance **automation** app. It helps individuals run their monthly financial administration with as little manual effort as possible: tracking what they owe, when it's due, and getting reminded before due dates pass.

The product is automation-first, not just a ledger. Wherever a user would otherwise repeat a manual step every month (re-entering a bill, recalculating an invoice, checking due dates), PayPilot should do it for them — predictably and without duplicates.

## Core Capabilities

- **Expense & bill tracking** — manual expenses plus recurring bills that auto-generate monthly transactions.
- **Credit card management** — purchases (including installments) assigned to the correct invoice using each card's closing and due day.
- **CSV import** — bulk-import transactions from bank/card statements with duplicate protection.
- **Due-date reminders** — proactive WhatsApp notifications for upcoming due dates, sent exactly once per bill/window.

## Target Use Cases

- A person consolidating recurring bills (rent, utilities, subscriptions) so they stop tracking them in spreadsheets.
- Someone who wants their credit card invoice composition to be accurate and predictable, including installment plans.
- A user who wants a nudge on WhatsApp before a bill is due, but never to be spammed.

## Value Proposition

The differentiator is **trustworthy automation of money admin**: correct invoice assignment, predictable installments, no duplicate transactions, and reminders that fire once and never for already-paid bills. Correctness of these financial rules is the product — see [[financial-rules]]. They are non-negotiable and must be protected by automated tests.
